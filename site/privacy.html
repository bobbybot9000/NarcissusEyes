Privacy Policy

Narcissus Eyes collects nothing.

Not "collects only what it needs", or "collects anonymized usage data". Nothing at all — and the app is built so that it could not, even by mistake.

What is collected

Nothing. Narcissus Eyes has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, and no advertising. No information about you or your Mac is gathered, stored, or transmitted.

The app is sandboxed with exactly one entitlement — camera access. It holds no networking entitlement of any kind, so there is no mechanism by which any data could leave your device.

How the camera is used

Narcissus Eyes shows you a live view of your own webcam. To crop that view to your face, it runs Apple's on-device Vision framework locally on your Mac.

Video frames are read from the camera, processed in memory, drawn to the small window on your screen, and immediately discarded. Frames are never recorded, saved to disk, uploaded, or shared with anyone — including the developer.

Your Mac shows its camera indicator light whenever the app is capturing, exactly as it does for any other app. Hiding the mirror from the menu bar stops capture entirely and turns the indicator off.

What is stored on your Mac

The app saves a small amount of local preference data so it behaves consistently between launches:

  • The position of the mirror on your screen
  • Which edge or display it was last docked to

This stays inside the app's sandbox container on your Mac. It contains no personal information and is never transmitted. Deleting the app removes it.

Third parties

There are none. Narcissus Eyes includes no third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers, or services. It communicates with no servers, including any operated by the developer.

Children

Narcissus Eyes is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date below. Should a future version of the app ever collect data — which is not planned — that change would be disclosed here and in the App Store privacy label before it ships.
