👀 LookNice

LookNice collects nothing.

Not "only what we need." Not "anonymized usage data." Nothing — and it's built so that it couldn't, even by accident.

No network

The app is sandboxed with exactly one permission: the camera. It holds no networking permission of any kind, so there is no route by which anything could leave your Mac. Not switched off. Not there.

The camera

LookNice shows you a live view of your own webcam and crops it to your face using Apple's Vision framework, running locally. Frames go from the camera to your screen and are thrown away. They are never recorded, saved, uploaded, or seen by anyone — including me.

Your Mac's camera light is on whenever the app is showing you, same as any other app. Hide the mirror from the menu bar and the camera stops completely.

What's stored

Where you left the mirror on screen, so it comes back to the same place. That sits in the app's own folder on your Mac, contains nothing personal, and goes away when you delete the app.

Nobody else

No third-party code, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts, no ads, no servers. There is nothing here to opt out of.
