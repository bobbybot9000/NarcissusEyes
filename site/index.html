Everyone looks at the screen. Nobody looks at the camera.

Narcissus Eyes docks a small mirror of your own webcam directly beneath the lens. The thing that pulls your eyes becomes the thing you ought to be looking at.

Download on the Mac App Store Under 1 MB · No account · No network

It follows your face

Not a fixed crop of the frame. On-device face tracking keeps you centered as you shift in your chair, lean in, or sit back.

Eyes mode

Shrinks the mirror to a thin strip framing just your eyes and brows. Enough to check your gaze. Small enough to forget it's there.

Invisible when you share

Excluded from screen capture at the window level. Share your screen and it simply isn't in the picture — no self-view echoing back at the room.

Knows your setup

Plug in a dock and it moves to the external display and camera on its own. Unplug and it returns to the built-in. Drag it across screens and it re-targets to match.

Hardware fit

It grows out of the notch

On MacBook Pro and MacBook Air, the mirror tucks directly beneath the notch and matches its exact width — read live from the display, not guessed per model. It reads as part of the hardware rather than a window sitting on top of it. Every other Mac docks flush to the top edge.

Privacy

No network access. Not restricted — absent.

Narcissus Eyes is sandboxed with exactly one entitlement: the camera. It has no networking entitlement of any kind, which means there is no code path by which a frame could leave your Mac — not disabled, not opt-in, not there.

Frames go from the camera, through on-device face detection, to the pixels on your screen, and are discarded. No account, no analytics, no telemetry, no server to trust. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and it is the whole truth rather than a careful one.

Support

Something not working?

I don't see it after installing

There's no Dock icon by design. Look for the eye in your menu bar, at the top right of the screen. The mirror itself sits at the top of your display, once you grant camera access.

It doesn't appear in screenshots

That's deliberate, and it's the point. The mirror is excluded from screen capture so it never leaks into a screen share. Look at the display itself rather than a capture.

The camera feed is black

Camera access was likely declined. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, enable Narcissus Eyes, then relaunch it from the menu bar.

It won't tuck under my notch

Notch docking needs a display that has one. On external monitors and older MacBooks the mirror docks flush to the top edge instead — that's expected behavior, not a fault.
