LOOK AT YOURSELF while looking like you're looking at them

LookNice parks a small mirror of your webcam directly under the lens. Your eyes go to it, because your eyes always go to it. It just happens to be exactly where the camera is.

Download for macOS Free · Under 1 MB · macOS 12+

Installing

Open the download and drag LookNice onto the Applications folder. It lives in your menu bar — there's no Dock icon, so look up and to the right.

The first-open ritual

macOS hasn't been introduced to this app yet, so it will act suspicious. Right-click LookNice in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Once only, then never again.

It sees nothing

No network access at all — not switched off, not there. Camera frames go to your screen and nowhere else. No account, no analytics, nothing leaves the machine.

Invisible on calls

The mirror is excluded from screen capture. Share your screen and it isn't in the picture, which rather spoils the trick otherwise.
